**Korean edition · 우리는 왜 사랑을 견디지 못하는가**

Love has to be reinvented.

Why can we long for love and still be afraid of getting close? Clinical psychologist Walt Odets draws on decades of practice, his own life, and the stories of gay men to trace how shame, grief, and the legacy of HIV/AIDS shape the ways people learn to love and protect themselves.

His accounts of men who care for one another and build relationships of their own invite us to rethink what intimacy can look like. The question of how gay men invent relationships opens onto a wider one: how can any of us come to understand and care for one another?
