# About Vitteum

Vitteum is a publisher drawn to the light that slips through the cracks in familiar ways of thinking.

We’re interested in the experiences and language of people who live outside familiar worlds. Across queer lives and relationships, psychology, the humanities, and art, we make books from stories we haven’t yet learned to see.

Understanding a life different from our own takes time. We believe a book can open a little space between us.
